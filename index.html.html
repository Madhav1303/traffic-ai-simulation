How it works

State: queue levels of the NS and EW approaches, current phase, and whether minimum green time has passed (400 states). Actions: keep or switch the phase. Reward: negative total queue length minus a small switching penalty. Update: Q(s,a) ← Q(s,a) + 0.1·[r + 0.9·max Q(s′,a′) − Q(s,a)]. One tick = 2 s; each green approach releases one car per tick; a switch costs one tick of lost (yellow) time.
